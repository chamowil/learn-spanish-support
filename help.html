Getting started

Onboarding asks which language you want explanations in, roughly how much Spanish you already have, and how much you want to do each day. There's an optional placement test — skip it and you can change your level later in Profile. Nothing is locked in; all of it is editable afterwards.

Which language does the app explain things in?

Chispa explains Spanish in English, German, French, Dutch, Norwegian, Swedish, Italian, Portuguese or Polish. Change it any time in Profile → My language; the whole interface, all lesson explanations and the pronunciation tips switch immediately — no restart. Pronunciation tips in particular are written for speakers of your language, so they call out the sounds that are actually hard for you.

The Spanish audio doesn't sound like where I'm learning

Chispa speaks one neutral Latin American Spanish throughout, deliberately: a single consistent accent is easier to learn from than a mixture. The Explore section covers how Spanish actually sounds in each of the 21 countries, including the words and pronunciations that differ.

Pronunciation scoring

  • Tap the microphone, wait for the countdown, then say the phrase clearly at a normal pace.
  • The score compares what Apple's speech recognition heard against the target phrase, word by word. Words shown in red are where it heard something different.
  • If scoring seems stuck or always zero, check that Chispa has microphone and speech recognition permission in Settings → Privacy & Security. Both are needed.
  • Background noise matters a lot. A quiet room changes results more than anything else.
  • It is a learning aid, not an examiner. A low score on a word you know you said correctly usually means the recognizer misheard, not that you were wrong.

Flashcards and why words come back

Words you meet in lessons enter a spaced-repetition schedule. Grading a card "Again" brings it back soon; "Good" and "Easy" push it further out. That is the point — the intervals are chosen to make you recall a word just as you're about to forget it, which is what makes it stick.

Streaks

A streak day counts once you've done any learning activity that day, in your device's time zone. It survives until the end of the day, so a late-night session still counts. Miss a full day and the streak resets — your longest streak is kept in Profile → Progress.

Subscription questions

  • What's free? A daily allowance of lessons and reviews, the first units, several game formats, and a limited number of pronunciation exercises, facts and country profiles.
  • What does Premium add? All units, unlimited lessons and reviews, the full Pronunciation Studio with tips and history, all conversation scenarios, every game format, and every fact and country profile.
  • I paid on my iPhone — do I pay again on my Mac? No. It's one subscription across iPhone, iPad and Mac. If Premium doesn't appear, tap Restore Purchases in Profile and make sure both devices use the same Apple Account.
  • How do I cancel? Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions on a Mac. Profile → Manage subscription takes you there. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • The paywall is empty or prices won't load. Almost always a temporary App Store connectivity problem. Close and reopen the app; if it persists, check you're signed into the App Store and email us.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings → Notifications → Chispa is allowed, and that Focus or Scheduled Summary isn't holding them back. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they keep working offline.

I found a mistake in the Spanish

Please tell us — this is the most useful email we get. Send the lesson or exercise name and what you'd expect instead, and mention the country or region you're thinking of, since a lot of "mistakes" are real regional differences worth calling out in the app.

Deleting your data

Profile has a reset that clears progress, and recordings can be deleted individually in Speak → Recordings. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
