Licenses

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Chispa bundles no third-party code. It is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks, so there are no open-source dependencies to attribute.

Apple frameworks used

  • SwiftUI — the entire interface, on all three platforms.
  • SwiftData — local storage of curriculum and learning progress.
  • StoreKit 2 — the Chispa Premium subscription.
  • AVFoundation / AVFAudio — Spanish speech synthesis, audio playback, and recording for pronunciation practice.
  • Speech — turning your recording into text so it can be scored, on-device wherever the device supports it.
  • GameKit — optional Game Center leaderboards.
  • AuthenticationServices — optional Sign in with Apple.
  • UserNotifications — locally scheduled daily reminders.
  • SF Symbols — all iconography, used under Apple's SF Symbols licence terms.

Content

Lessons, vocabulary, quiz questions, conversation scenarios, country profiles, history articles and "Did You Know?" facts are original work written for Chispa, © 2026 Wuilmer Chamo. Historical and cultural material is compiled from public reference sources; where a specific fact has a source worth naming, the app shows it on the card itself.

Country flags are rendered from Unicode flag characters, not bitmap artwork. The app icon is original work created for Chispa.

Spanish audio

Spanish speech is generated on your device by Apple's speech synthesiser (AVSpeechSynthesizer). No licensed voice recordings are bundled. Any recording you make of your own voice stays on your device.

Chispa is an independent Spanish-learning app.
