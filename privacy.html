Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The short version. Chispa has no accounts you must create, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Your lessons, progress, streaks and voice recordings stay on your device. Nothing is sent to a server we run, because there isn't one.

Who we are

Chispa is developed and published by Wuilmer Chamo. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights below, email chamowil@outlook.com.

What stays on your device

  • Learning data — lessons completed, quiz answers, flashcard review schedule, XP, streaks and badges.
  • Your profile — the display name you type during onboarding, your chosen level, your daily goal and your chosen explanation language.
  • Voice recordings — if you choose to save a take in the Pronunciation Studio, the audio file is written to the app's own storage on your device. You can delete recordings from Speak → Recordings at any time.
  • Settings — reminder times and notification preferences.

All of this lives in the app's private container. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also clear progress from inside the app without deleting it.

Microphone and speech recognition

Pronunciation scoring needs to hear you. When you tap the record button, Chispa captures audio and passes it to Apple's Speech framework to turn it into text, which is then compared against the target phrase to produce a score. Chispa requests on-device recognition wherever the device supports it. On devices or languages where it doesn't, Apple may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's own privacy policy. Chispa never sends your audio anywhere else, and never stores it outside your device.

The microphone is used only while you are actively recording in the Pronunciation Studio.

Purchases

Chispa Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple. Payment, billing and renewals are handled entirely by the App Store. Chispa receives from Apple only whether a subscription is currently active — no card details, no name, no email address.

Optional features

  • Sign in with Apple — optional, and used only to keep progress associated with you across your own devices. Apple lets you hide your real email address; Chispa does not need it and does not use it to contact you.
  • iCloud sync — if enabled, your learning data is mirrored to your own private iCloud database. It is your iCloud account and your storage; Apple acts as the processor and we cannot read it.
  • Game Center — if you sign in, your XP score is submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Declining leaves the leaderboard local to your device.
  • Notifications — daily reminders and the daily fact are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No Google, Meta, or similar SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking as the App Store defines it: nothing about you is linked with data from other companies or apps.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. Under the CCPA/CPRA: we do not sell or share personal information, and we have no "Do Not Sell" flow to offer because there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Chispa is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing and requires no account, a child can use it without any data leaving the device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and comparable laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because Chispa holds no personal data on any server we control, these rights are exercised directly on your device: view and edit your profile in the app, delete recordings in Speak → Recordings, clear progress in Profile, or delete the app to remove everything. If you have signed in with Apple or enabled iCloud sync and want that data removed, email chamowil@outlook.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Legal basis for the limited processing that does occur: performance of a contract (providing the app and its subscription features) and your consent (microphone access, notifications, iCloud, Game Center — each of which you can withdraw in iOS or macOS Settings at any time).

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the new version will appear here before the corresponding app update ships.
