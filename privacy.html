Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version. Chispa has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. Your lessons, progress, streaks and settings stay on your device. Nothing is sent to a server we run, because there isn't one. This policy covers Chispa on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Who we are

Chispa is developed and published by Wuilmer Chamo. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights below, email chamowil@outlook.com.

What stays on your device

  • Learning data — lessons completed, quiz answers, flashcard review schedule, XP, streaks and badges.
  • Your profile — the display name you type during onboarding, your level, your daily goal and your chosen explanation language.
  • Pronunciation and conversation history — the score and the recognized text of each pronunciation attempt, and the transcripts of conversations you complete. Chispa does not save audio recordings.
  • Settings — reminder times and notification preferences.

All of this lives in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it without deleting the app: Profile → Delete my data.

Microphone and speech recognition

Pronunciation scoring needs to hear you. When you tap the record button, Chispa asks your device's speech recognizer to turn your voice into text, then compares that text with the target phrase to produce a score.

  • On iPhone and iPad the recognizer is Apple's Speech framework. Chispa requests on-device recognition where the device supports it; otherwise Apple may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy.
  • On Android the recognizer is provided by the device's speech service (normally Google's). Depending on the device, language and settings, audio may be processed on the device or sent to Google under Google's privacy policy.

In both cases the audio goes to the operating system's speech service, not to Chispa's developer. Chispa never receives, stores or forwards your audio, and never sends it anywhere else. The microphone is used only while you are actively recording in a pronunciation exercise, a lesson's “Say it” card or a conversation. This is why Google Play's Data safety section lists voice or sound recordings as data that is processed ephemerally and optional.

Purchases

Chispa Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple (App Store) on iPhone/iPad or by Google (Google Play) on Android, with a free trial for new subscribers. Payment, billing, renewals and refunds are handled entirely by that store. Chispa receives from the store only whether a subscription is currently active — no card details, no name and no email address.

Notifications

Daily reminders, “streak at risk” nudges and the daily fact are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server, and notifications only appear if you allow them in system settings.

Links and sharing

Links in the app (this policy, the terms, subscription management) open in your browser or the store app. If you use a share button, your device's own share sheet handles what you send and to whom; Chispa does not see it.

Device backups

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone/iPad, Android Backup on Android) may include the app's data, according to your backup settings. That is between you and Apple or Google; Chispa's developer cannot see it.

Features that may send text off your device

The current version runs its conversation partner entirely on your device. If a future version offers an online conversation partner, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version ships, and it will ask for your consent first.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No Google Analytics, Firebase, Meta or similar SDKs.
  • No tracking as Apple and Google define it: nothing about you is linked with data from other companies or apps.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. Under the CCPA/CPRA: we do not sell or share personal information, and we have no “Do Not Sell” flow to offer because there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Chispa is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app collects nothing and requires no account, a child can use it without any data leaving the device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR and comparable laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because Chispa holds no personal data on any server we control, these rights are exercised directly on your device: view and edit your profile in the app, erase your learning data with Profile → Delete my data, or delete the app to remove everything. If you want help or believe we hold data about you, email chamowil@outlook.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Legal basis for the limited processing that does occur: performance of a contract (providing the app and its subscription features) and your consent (microphone access, speech recognition, notifications — each of which you can withdraw in your device's system settings at any time).

Earlier native iPhone and iPad versions

Before the cross-platform release, Chispa was available on Apple devices as a native iPhone, iPad and Mac app. If you are still running that earlier version, the following optional features applied to it and are governed by the same principles above:

  • Saved voice takes — if you chose to save a take in the Pronunciation Studio, the audio file was written to the app's own storage on your device, and you could delete it from Speak → Recordings. The current cross-platform version does not save audio at all.
  • Sign in with Apple — optional, used only to keep progress associated with you across your own devices. Apple lets you hide your real email address; Chispa did not need it.
  • iCloud sync — if enabled, your learning data was mirrored to your own private iCloud database. It is your iCloud account and your storage; we cannot read it.
  • Game Center — if you signed in, your XP score was submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname.

If you used any of these and want the related data removed, email chamowil@outlook.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the new version will appear here before the corresponding app update ships.
